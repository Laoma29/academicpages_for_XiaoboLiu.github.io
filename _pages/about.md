I am a postdoctoral researcher in computational neuroscience and AI, with research interests spanning brain dynamics, computational psychiatry, neuro-AI, and brain-computer interfaces. My work focuses on understanding the spatiotemporal organization of human brain activity by integrating multimodal neuroimaging, computational modeling, complex systems theory, and machine learning.

During my PhD at the [Montreal Neurological Institute](https://www.mcgill.ca/neuro/), McGill University, under the supervision of [Prof. Sylvain Baillet](https://www.neurospeed-bailletlab.org/), I investigated the spatiotemporal structure of spontaneous traveling waves in the human brain. My research combined MEG, EEG, fMRI, computational modeling, and deep learning to uncover fundamental principles of large-scale brain dynamics and their alterations in psychiatric and neurodevelopmental disorders.

My broader research framework connects three interdisciplinary areas: computational neuroscience, computational psychiatry, and neuro-AI. By bridging biomedical foundations in brain science and mental health with computational approaches in modeling, theory, and tool development, I aim to reverse-engineer neural mechanisms, model neural circuit function, develop computational tools for psychiatric diagnosis and treatment, and inspire brain-based AI architectures.

I am currently collaborating with [Prof. Ben Fulcher](https://www.benfulcher.com/), [Prof. James M. Shine](https://www.jamesmshine.com/), and [Prof. Joseph Lizier](https://lizier.me/joseph/) on projects that integrate neurophysics, complex systems, information dynamics, and computational neuroscience. This work extends my previous studies of cortical traveling waves, MEG/fMRI-based brain dynamics, cortical gradients, and brain disorders by applying advanced time-series analysis, information-theoretic measures, and complex-network modeling to characterize how large-scale neural activity supports cognition, arousal, and brain health.

Previously, I received my Master's degree in Biomedical Engineering from the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/) under the supervision of [Prof. Dezhong Yao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ClUoWqsAAAAJ&hl=en), and my Bachelor's degree in Automation from [HuaQiao University](https://eng.hqu.edu.cn/).

**Research Interests:** Computational Neuroscience | Computational Psychiatry | Neuro-AI | Complex Systems | Brain-Computer Interfaces
