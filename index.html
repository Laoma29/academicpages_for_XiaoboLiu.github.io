About Me

I am a computational neuroscientist and AI researcher studying the spatiotemporal dynamics of human brain activity. My work integrates MEG, EEG, fMRI, computational modeling, and machine learning to understand large-scale brain dynamics and their links to psychiatric and neurodevelopmental disorders.

I completed my PhD at the Montreal Neurological Institute, McGill University, supervised by Prof. Sylvain Baillet. I am now a postdoctoral researcher working across computational neuroscience, neurophysics, information dynamics, and complex systems with Prof. Ben Fulcher, Prof. James M. Shine, and Prof. Joseph Lizier.

Previously, I received my Master's degree in Biomedical Engineering from UESTC under Prof. Dezhong Yao, and my Bachelor's degree in Automation from HuaQiao University.

My broader research framework connects computational neuroscience, computational psychiatry, and neuro-AI: reverse-engineering neural mechanisms, modeling brain disorders, and building interpretable computational tools inspired by brain dynamics.
